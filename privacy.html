Cash Diamond Privacy Policy

Cash Diamond Baseball is made by BIRCO, Inc. Last updated October 24, 2026.

The short version

We don't collect personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress, cash, shop items and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Online Vs

When you play a friend with a game code, the nickname you type and your game moves pass through our game server so the two phones can play. The server keeps them in memory only while the room is open (at most 3 hours) and does not save them. It briefly uses your internet address to stop abuse, and forgets it within 10 minutes. There is no chat.

Purchases

The Full Game is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play (or Stripe on the web version). They handle payment, and we never see card details. A grown-up check appears before any purchase.

Children

Cash Diamond is made for all ages, including kids. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't collect it from children under 13. The cash in the game is pretend money. It can't be bought, cashed out or traded.
