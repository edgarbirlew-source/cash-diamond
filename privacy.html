Cash Diamond Privacy Policy

Cash Diamond Baseball is made by BIRCO, Inc. Last updated November 18, 2026.

The short version

BIRCO doesn't collect personal information itself. There are no accounts and no analytics. The Apple App Store and Google Play versions contain optional video ads from Google AdMob; buying the Full Game removes all ads.

Ads (store apps only)

After a game, a player may choose to tap "Watch a video: double it". Nothing plays by itself and no ad ever shows during play. The video comes from Google AdMob. To show it, AdMob may collect device information such as an advertising identifier, your internet address and how the ad was viewed. On iPhone, ads are only personalized if you allow tracking when asked; you can say no and still get every reward. Where the law requires it, "Ad privacy choices" under More from BIRCO lets you change your choice. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's advertising policy. The web version has no ads. Full Game owners never see an ad.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress, cash, shop items and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Online Vs

When you play a friend with a game code, the nickname you type and your game moves pass through our game server so the two phones can play. The server keeps them in memory only while the room is open (at most 3 hours) and does not save them. It briefly uses your internet address to stop abuse, and forgets it within 10 minutes. There is no chat.

Purchases

The Full Game is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play (or Stripe on the web version). They handle payment, and we never see card details. A grown-up check appears before any purchase.

Children

Cash Diamond is made for all ages, including kids. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't collect it from children under 13. The cash in the game is pretend money. It can't be bought, cashed out or traded.
